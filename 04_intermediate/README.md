# Intermediate data

`demo/stage_2/` contains the processed 100 m model layers and adopted settings. `demo/stage_3/` contains destination attractiveness, destination-population allocations and in-transit population.

The [Stage 2 dictionary](demo/stage_2/README.md) and [Stage 3 dictionary](demo/stage_3/README.md) describe their files and units. Original sources are under `01_inputs/demo/raw/`; final products are under `05_outputs/demo/`.

[Time use: derivation of hourly location shares](time_use.md) documents survey processing, day-type transfer, corrections, parameter sources and the supplied Finland and Japan profiles.
